The impact of glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists on objective physical activity in adults: a systematic review and exploratory meta-analysis.
Heeyeon Kim, Ji Heon Chae, Hyo Youl Moon — International journal of obesity (2005) Jul 2026
🏥 PopulationAdults with overweight or obesity
💊 MedicationsGLP-1 receptor agonists
🌍 Country / DatasetMultiple (PubMed and Embase search)
🔬 Key FindingsThis systematic review and exploratory meta-analysis evaluated the effect of GLP-1RA treatment on objectively measured physical activity in adults with overweight or obesity. The review aims to clarify whether GLP-1RAs influence PA beyond their weight reduction effects, which is important for understanding long-term energy balance and sustainability of treatment benefits.

💊 MedicationsGLP-1 receptor agonists vs dipeptidyl peptidase-4 inhibitors
🌍 Country / DatasetChina (36 hospitals, electronic medical records)

💊 MedicationsCagriSema, semaglutide, cagrilintide, and tirzepatide
🌍 Country / DatasetMultiple (randomized clinical trials)
