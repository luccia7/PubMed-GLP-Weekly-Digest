Eugene E Wright, Eileen Huang, Anila Bindal — Diabetes technology & therapeutics Sep 2026
🏥 PopulationType 2 diabetes patients on non-intensive therapy initiating GLP-1 RAs
💊 MedicationsGLP-1 receptor agonists; continuous glucose monitoring
🌍 Country / DatasetInovalon database (US)
🔬 Key FindingsAmong 934 adherent CGM users matched with 3,747 non-CGM users, 68.4% of CGM users continued GLP-1 RA at one year post-initiation. Study demonstrates association between CGM utilization and improved GLP-1 RA adherence in type 2 diabetes patients.
