Systematic review and meta-analysis 👥 Not reported
Efficacy and safety of orforglipron in obesity with type 2 diabetes mellitus: a GRADE-assessed meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
Ahmed Emara, Mohamed Emara, Ahmed Mansour, et al. — Diabetology & metabolic syndrome Jun 2026
🏥 PopulationAdults with obesity and type 2 diabetes mellitus
💊 MedicationsOrforglipron vs placebo
🌍 Country / DatasetMulti-database (PubMed, Scopus, Cochrane CENTRAL, Web of Science)
🔬 Key FindingsSystematic review and meta-analysis evaluated orforglipron, a novel oral GLP-1 receptor agonist developed to improve adherence compared with injectable therapies, for efficacy and safety in obesity with T2DM. The study pooled data from RCTs comparing orforglipron with placebo using random-effects models through January 2026.

Efficacy and tolerance of GLP-1 analogues in the treatment of adolescent obesity: analysis of a French population.
Sarah Bouhmouch, Béatrice Dubern, Catherine Terraz, et al. — Archives de pediatrie : organe officiel de la Societe francaise de pediatrie Jun 2026
🏥 PopulationObese adolescents without diabetes
💊 MedicationsGLP-1 analogues
🌍 Country / DatasetFrance
🔬 Key FindingsAnalysis of a French population evaluated efficacy and tolerance of GLP-1 analogues in adolescent obesity treatment. The study addresses a poorly studied area as GLP-1 analogues, proven effective in adults, are considered therapeutic alternatives for obese adolescents despite limited efficacy and tolerance data in this age group without diabetes.

Effects of Weight-Loss Interventions on Kidney, Cardiometabolic and Anthropometric Outcomes in Adults With CKD and Overweight or Obesity: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Anastasia Stamatiou, Marieta P Theodorakopoulou, Fotini Iatridi, et al. — Diabetes, obesity & metabolism Jun 2026
🏥 PopulationAdults with chronic kidney disease and overweight or obesity
💊 MedicationsWeight-loss interventions (including GLP-1 based therapies)
🌍 Country / DatasetMulti-database (PubMed, CENTRAL, Embase)
🔬 Key FindingsUpdated systematic review and meta-analysis evaluated effects of weight-loss interventions on kidney, cardiometabolic and anthropometric outcomes in overweight or obese adults with CKD through August 2025.
