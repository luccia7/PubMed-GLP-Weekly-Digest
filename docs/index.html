Systematic review and meta-analysis 👥 60 articles, n=1,250,717
GLP-1 Receptor Agonists and Musculoskeletal Outcomes: A Systematic Literature Review and Meta-Analysis.
Charlotte Beaudart, Yveline Malréchauffé, Sophie van Heden, et al. — Drugs Oct 2026
🏥 PopulationType 2 diabetes and obesity patients treated with GLP-1 receptor agonists
💊 MedicationsGLP-1 receptor agonists
🌍 Country / DatasetNot specified
🔬 Key FindingsThis meta-analysis of 60 articles including 46 RCTs, 13 real-world evidence studies, and 1 pharmacovigilance study involving over 1.2 million individuals systematically reviewed the effects of GLP-1 RAs on bone, muscle, and joint outcomes. The comprehensive synthesis characterizes musculoskeletal health effects of increasingly used GLP-1 RAs for type 2 diabetes and obesity treatment.
