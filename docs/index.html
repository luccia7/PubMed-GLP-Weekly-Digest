Retrospective cohort study 👥 Not reported
Effect of two-week perioperative glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist interruption on retained gastric contents during esophagogastroduodenoscopy: a retrospective, observational study.
Zhiyu Dong, Ouyang Li, Jiajun Wu, et al. — Surgical endoscopy Jul 2026
🏥 PopulationPatients undergoing elective esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD) between August 2023 and May 2025
💊 MedicationsGLP-1 receptor agonists (various, not specified) vs no GLP-1RA use
🌍 Country / DatasetSingle-center study, location not specified
🔬 Key FindingsStudy examined the effect of two-week GLP-1RA discontinuation on retained gastric contents during EGD. GLP-1RAs slow gastric emptying and may increase retained gastric contents risk. Outcomes measured included presence of retained gastric contents in three exposure groups: no GLP-1RA use, continued GLP-1RA use, and two-week GLP-1RA hold.
Retrospective cohort study 👥 Not reported
Association of GLP-1RA discontinuation and risk of depressive and anxiety disorders in people with type 2 diabetes: a cohort study.
Tingyu Zhang, Ping He, Yunxi Ji, et al. — Nature metabolism Jul 2026
🏥 PopulationPeople with type 2 diabetes
💊 MedicationsGLP-1 receptor agonists vs dipeptidyl peptidase-4 inhibitors (DPP4is) or sodium-glucose cotransporter-2 inhibitors
🌍 Country / DatasetShanghai Hospital Link Database
🔬 Key FindingsGLP-1RA discontinuation was associated with increased risk of depressive and anxiety disorders in people with type 2 diabetes. The study examined incident psychiatric events during active treatment and after cessation, revealing psychiatric safety concerns not previously well-characterized after GLP-1RA discontinuation.
Retrospective cohort study 👥 n=10,649
Patterns of Use for GLP-1 Receptor Agonists in a Weight Management Cohort: A Military Health System Database Analysis of Active Duty Service Members From 2021 to 2025.
Luke B Miller, Matthew J Dellen, Ester J Gilbert, et al. — Military medicine Jul 2026
🏥 PopulationActive duty service members (ADSMs) initiating GLP-1RA therapy for weight management between January 1, 2021, and December 31, 2025
💊 MedicationsLiraglutide (Saxenda), semaglutide (Wegovy), tirzepatide (Zepbound)
🌍 Country / DatasetMilitary Health System (MHS), United States
🔬 Key FindingsThis real-world analysis examined persistence, adherence, and predictors of discontinuation among new users of different GLP-1RAs in active duty service members. Study aimed to compare long-term persistence patterns and identify factors associated with drug discontinuation across the three GLP-1RA formulations.
Meta-analysis 👥 9 RCTs, 595 participants
Glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists for weight management in mental illness: a network meta-analysis.
Taro Kishi, Toshikazu Ikuta, Kenji Sakuma, et al. — Translational psychiatry Jul 2026
🏥 PopulationAdults with obesity comorbid with mental illness (average age 37.8 years, 55.3% female, 83.9% schizophrenia spectrum disorders)
💊 MedicationsGLP-1 receptor agonists (various)
🌍 Country / DatasetMultiple countries
🔬 Key FindingsNetwork meta-analysis of nine randomized controlled trials evaluated comparative risk-benefit profiles of GLP-1RAs in individuals with obesity and mental illness. Primary outcome was body weight change; secondary outcomes included BMI, waist circumference, and various blood-based metabolic measures (fasting plasma glucose, HbA1c, cholesterol, triglycerides).
Cross-sectional study 👥 n=1,693
GLP-1 medications: use and interest in a representative survey of Finns.
Piia Tuuli Jallinoja, Kirsi H Pietiläinen, Virginia W Chang — International journal of obesity (2005) Jul 2026
🏥 PopulationNationally representative sample of Finnish adults
💊 MedicationsGLP-1-based medications (semaglutide, tirzepatide)
🌍 Country / DatasetFinland
🔬 Key FindingsNationally representative online survey conducted in June 2025 assessed GLP-1 medication use and interest among Finnish adults. Study examined who uses or considers these medications, particularly across socioeconomic groups, BMI categories, and weight management experiences, providing data on real-world adoption patterns.
Systematic review and meta-analysis 👥 Multiple RCTs
Efficacy and Safety of the Dual Glucagon-Like Peptide-1 and Glucagon Receptor Agonist Mazdutide in Predominantly Chinese Adults With Obesity and/or Type 2 Diabetes: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
A B M Kamrul-Hasan, Subhankar Chatterjee, Hamid Ashraf, et al. — Diabetes, obesity & metabolism Jul 2026
🏥 PopulationAdults with overweight/obesity and/or type 2 diabetes, predominantly Chinese
💊 MedicationsMazdutide (dual GLP-1 and glucagon receptor agonist) vs placebo or active comparators
🌍 Country / DatasetMultiple sources including PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, ClinicalTrials.gov, search date February 20, 2026
🔬 Key FindingsSystematic review and meta-analysis assessed efficacy and safety of mazdutide in predominantly Chinese adults with overweight/obesity and/or type 2 diabetes. Co-primary outcomes were percent change in body weight and change in HbA1c; secondary outcomes included other weight-related and metabolic outcomes plus adverse events.
