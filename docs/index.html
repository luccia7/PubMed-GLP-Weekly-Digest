Systematic review and meta-analysis 👥 Not reported
Nonadherence to GLP-1 Receptor Agonists Among Individuals With Overweight or Obesity: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials.
Efrata A Shegena, Mekdes Kiflu, Muktar Ahmed, et al. — Obesity reviews : an official journal of the International Association for the Study of Obesity Jul 2026
🏥 PopulationIndividuals with overweight or obesity with or without diabetes participating in RCTs
💊 MedicationsGLP-1 receptor agonists
🌍 Country / DatasetNot reported
🔬 Key FindingsThis systematic review and meta-analysis evaluated nonadherence rates and reasons for discontinuation of GLP-1 agonists in obese/overweight populations enrolled in randomized controlled trials. The study aimed to estimate pooled nonadherence rates and identify patterns of treatment persistence, with adverse drug reactions being a key factor limiting treatment continuation.

Therapeutic role of semaglutide in metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease and metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis: A systematic review and meta-analysis of placebo-controlled trials with GRADE evidence assessment.
Suleman Khan, Asad Jamal, Maria Qadri, et al. — Medicine Jul 2026
🏥 PopulationAdults with metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) or steatohepatitis (MASH)
💊 MedicationsSemaglutide versus placebo
🌍 Country / DatasetNot reported
🔬 Key FindingsThis systematic review and meta-analysis with GRADE evidence assessment evaluated the efficacy and tolerability of subcutaneous semaglutide versus placebo in adults with NAFLD/NASH, focusing on biochemical, histologic, metabolic outcomes and tolerability as potential disease-modifying therapy for this leading cause of chronic liver disease.
Retrospective cohort study 👥 n=292
Mortality and persistence in therapy in elderly subjects with type 2 diabetes receiving gliflozins or incretins.
Alice Del Zoppo, Diego Moriconi, Eleni Rebelos, et al. — Journal of endocrinological investigation Jul 2026
🏥 PopulationElderly subjects aged ≥80 years with type 2 diabetes (155 on SGLT2i, 137 on GLP-1 RA)
💊 MedicationsSodium-glucose cotransporter 2 inhibitors (SGLT2i) and glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1 RA)
🌍 Country / DatasetNot reported
🔬 Key FindingsThis real-world retrospective study evaluated treatment persistence and mortality outcomes in 292 elderly patients aged ≥80 years with type 2 diabetes initiating SGLT2i or GLP-1 RA after age 75, stratified by age at treatment initiation, with primary outcomes including all-cause mortality, treatment persistence, and longitudinal renal function changes.
