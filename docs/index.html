GLP-1 Receptor Agonists Are Associated With Lower Early Revision Risk Compared to Bariatric Surgery Following Total Hip Arthroplasty: A Retrospective Cohort Study.
Smit Brahmbhatt, Amal Mathew, Seth Shoap, et al. — HSS journal : the musculoskeletal journal of Hospital for Special Surgery Oct 2026
🏥 PopulationAdults aged ≥18 years undergoing total hip arthroplasty with obesity
💊 MedicationsGLP-1 receptor agonists vs bariatric surgery
🌍 Country / DatasetTriNetX Global Collaborative Network
🔬 Key FindingsGLP-1 receptor agonists were associated with lower early postoperative revision risk compared to prior bariatric surgery in total hip arthroplasty patients with obesity. Differences in complications persisted at mid-term follow-up, suggesting GLP-1 RAs may be a safer alternative for weight management in this surgical population.

Association between Socioeconomic Deprivation and Clinical Outcomes of Glucagon-Like Peptide-1 Receptor Agonists in Heart Failure.
Shaheer Qureshi, Maryam Sajid, Habib R Khan, et al. — The American journal of cardiology Oct 2026
🏥 PopulationAdults with heart failure stratified by socioeconomic deprivation status
💊 MedicationsGLP-1 receptor agonists
🌍 Country / DatasetTriNetX Global Network (2020-2024)
🔬 Key FindingsSocioeconomic deprivation was associated with differential outcomes of GLP-1 receptor agonist therapy in heart failure patients. Among GLP-1RA users, residual disparities in clinical effectiveness and safety outcomes persisted across socioeconomic strata even after propensity-score matching.

Short-term clinical efficacy and recorded adverse events of semaglutide combined with dapagliflozin in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus and chronic kidney disease.
Cheng Gong, Qiong Luo, Qin Li — Medicine Oct 2026
🏥 PopulationPatients with type 2 diabetes mellitus complicated by chronic kidney disease
💊 MedicationsSemaglutide combined with dapagliflozin
🌍 Country / DatasetSingle hospital (Japan), March 2023 to unspecified date
🔬 Key FindingsCombined semaglutide and dapagliflozin therapy demonstrated short-term clinical efficacy in T2DM patients with CKD, with documented metabolic and cardiorenal benefits. Adverse events were recorded and analyzed to characterize safety profile of this dual-agent approach.

Real-World Adherence Patterns and Predictors of GLP-1 Receptor Agonist Use for Obesity Management in a Medicaid Population.
Shadi Bazzazzadehgan, Eric Pittman, Liang-Yuan Lin, et al. — Advances in therapy Oct 2026
🏥 PopulationMississippi Medicaid members aged ≥18 years with obesity initiating GLP-1 RAs
💊 MedicationsGLP-1 receptor agonists
🌍 Country / DatasetMississippi Medicaid claims data (January 2023 to December 2025)
🔬 Key FindingsReal-world adherence and persistence patterns to GLP-1 receptor agonist anti-obesity medications were examined in a Medicaid population following statewide coverage initiation in July 2023 and medication shortage resolution. Specific predictors of adherence and persistence were identified to characterize treatment patterns in this vulnerable population.
