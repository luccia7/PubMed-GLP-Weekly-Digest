Ahmed M Ashour — Diabetes, metabolic syndrome and obesity : targets and therapy 2026
🏥 PopulationPatients with type 2 diabetes mellitus
💊 MedicationsInjectable and oral GLP-1 receptor agonists (oral semaglutide vs injectable formulations)
🌍 Country / DatasetMultiple sources: PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, real-world observational data

GLP-1 receptor agonist therapy and incident atrial fibrillation in non-diabetic adults with obesity and chronic kidney disease: a propensity matched cohort study.
Abdulraheem Hassan, Efeturi Okorigba, Chilaka Grace, et al. — International journal of cardiology Oct 2026
🏥 PopulationNon-diabetic adults aged ≥18 years with obesity and chronic kidney disease stages 3-5, diagnosed 2021-2024
💊 MedicationsSemaglutide, liraglutide, tirzepatide vs no GLP-1 RA
🌍 Country / DatasetTriNetX US Collaborative Network (66 healthcare organizations)
🔬 Key FindingsTarget trial emulation assessed whether GLP-1RA therapy reduces 1-year atrial fibrillation incidence in non-diabetic adults with obesity and CKD. Study examined incident AF in this understudied cardiorenal phenotype using propensity-score matching across multiple US healthcare organizations.
Real-world study 👥 Over 1,000,000 adults
Real-World Treatment Patterns and Weight Outcomes Before and After a Tirzepatide Price Increase in Over One Million Self-Paying UK Adults With Overweight or Obesity.
Sophie Dix, Anna Mulligan, Mattia Ficarelli, et al. — Diabetes, obesity & metabolism Oct 2026
🏥 PopulationSelf-paying UK adults with overweight or obesity
💊 MedicationsSemaglutide, tirzepatide
🌍 Country / DatasetUnited Kingdom (telehealth provider)
🔬 Key FindingsRetrospective cohort study characterized treatment patterns and weight outcomes before and after a September 2025 tirzepatide price increase of up to 170% in over one million self-funded UK adults. Study assessed provider reach, treatment switching patterns, and longitudinal weight trajectories in response to relative pricing changes.

GLP-1 Receptor Agonist Therapy Is Associated With Lower Risk of Lower Extremity Amputation in Adults With Type 2 Diabetes and Foot Ulcer Without Coded Peripheral Arterial Disease: A Hypothesis-Generating Real-World Cohort Study.
Hani Robinson, Mustafa Yassin, Dror Robinson, et al. — Diabetes, obesity & metabolism Oct 2026
🏥 PopulationAdults with type 2 diabetes and diabetic foot ulcer
💊 MedicationsGLP-1 receptor agonists vs other glucose-lowering therapy
🌍 Country / DatasetTriNetX Global Collaborative Network
🔬 Key FindingsNew-user, active-comparator retrospective cohort study compared lower extremity amputation risk in T2DM patients with diabetic foot ulcer treated with GLP-1 RAs versus other glucose-lowering therapy. Study tested whether coded peripheral arterial disease modifies this association and examined replication across different comparator classes.
